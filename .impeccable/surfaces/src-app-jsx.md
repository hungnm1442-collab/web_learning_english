# Learning Dashboard & Path Surface Brief

- **Scope:** `src/App.jsx` & `src/App.css` (Learning workspace shell, Learning Path Bento view, Flashcards, Learned Words, Settings; `LandingPage` preserved untouched)
- **Visitor Mode:** Operate

## Audience & Task
Vietnamese self-learners at A2 level opening their daily study dashboard to resume their current lesson (`Present simple`), check their weekly study rhythm and schedule, review due vocabulary flashcards, and track mastery across core skills.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm frosted-glass bento workspace (Glassy Dashboard V24 architecture) fused with LingoLy's signature Wine Red, Quiet Blue, and Warm Beige palette—turning daily English study into a tactile, luminous studio board rather than a flat list of lessons.

OWN-WORLD: Translucent warm champagne-beige glass panels (`rgba(255, 250, 246, 0.72)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`) and a floating smoked-wine glass sidebar over a rich wine-to-sand ambient canvas (`#5e1929` to `#d8c5bc`), anchored by a saturated Wine-Red hero card (`#8d1e32` to `#b82d46`), Quiet Blue (`#46658b`) data accents, and Manrope + Noto Serif typography.

STORY: The learner sees their daily momentum at a glance (68% A2 path completion, 12 words due, 7-day streak), picks today's lesson from the weekly calendar & lesson schedule card, or jumps straight into flashcards.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating frosted-wine left sidebar (`264px`) alongside a rounded frosted-beige main board. Top bar shows a personalized Vietnamese greeting, date/attention summary, and two pill actions (`Ôn Flashcard` light glass pill + `+ Học bài tiếp` dark wine pill). Upper bento row splits into the Wine-Red Hero Card (status pill, serif-accented headline, 3 mini-metrics, 2 CTA pills, and a 68% segmented circular gauge with 5 orbiting skill badges) and the Frosted Calendar & Lesson Schedule Card (big date `08`, 7-day week strip, and interactive lesson schedule rows). Bottom bento row holds 4 KPI cards with SVG sparklines (86 words learned, 4/4 active lessons, 7-day streak in highlighted Wine-Red gradient, and 92.4% retention rate).

FORM: User-pinned Glassy Dashboard V24 bento layout translated into LingoLy's Wine Red + Quiet Blue + Warm Beige palette (position 1, user-pinned; `seed: none (unavailable)`).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
